Get started

  1. Sign in with Kakao

    Open magomap and tap “Sign in with Kakao”. For now, only invited accounts can use it.

  2. Sync from Kakao Map

    On a computer, add the magomap extension to Chrome and sign in to Kakao Map in the same browser. Then, in magomap, open Account and choose “Sync from Kakao Map”. Syncing again never changes the notes you wrote here.

  3. Add to your home screen (iPhone)

    Open magomap in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

  4. Add to your home screen (Android)

    Open magomap in Chrome, open the menu, then “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
