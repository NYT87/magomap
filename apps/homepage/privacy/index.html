Privacy

magomap stores only what the map needs.

What's stored

  • Your Kakao user id and nickname (to sign you in)
  • Your saved places, lists, visit notes and links
  • Previous locations of places
  • Sign-in sessions (tokens are stored only as hashes)

How to delete it

In the app, go to Account → Delete account. Your data is removed from the server and from that device. Kakao Map and your Kakao account aren't touched.

No tracking

This homepage has no tracking, no analytics and no cookies.

magomap is an independent personal project. It isn't affiliated with Kakao.
