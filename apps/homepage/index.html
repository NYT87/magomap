Shape your own map.

magomap keeps the places you save and your memories of them together. Write a note each time you visit, like a diary, collect links, and sort places into lists. It keeps working where the signal is weak.

Open magomap Get started

About the name

The name comes from Mago Halmi (마고할미), the giant creation goddess of Korean mythology who shaped the mountains, rivers and islands. In magomap, everyone shapes their own personal geography: the places they keep, and what they remember about them.

What you can do

  • Map

    Your saved places at a glance, in their list colours. Turn lists on and off.

  • Visit diary

    Write a dated note every time you go. Past visits stack up like a diary.

  • Links

    Keep reviews, menus and blog posts with each place.

  • Lists

    Make your own lists. One place can be in many lists.

  • Kakao import

    Bring in your Kakao Map lists, and lists you follow, through kakao-list. Your notes here are never changed.

  • Offline

    Write notes without a connection; they sync on their own when you're back.

  • Previous locations

    When a place moves, its old spot stays on the map, faded, so you know where a memory happened.
